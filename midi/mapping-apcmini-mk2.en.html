Three zones in the grid

Rows 1 to 3 : effects. Rows 4 to 6 : palettes. Rows 7 and 8 : eight solid colours, which stand in for the custom palette — one press puts the deck on that colour.

Reading the numbers

Pads carry their note number, faders their CC number. On the grid : note = row from the bottom × 8 + column, and the bottom-left pad is 0.

Measured on the hardware, not taken from the documentation.

Live and master functions

The bottom pads carry their real colour. Deck A left of the split, deck B right.

Effects light dim white at rest and blinking white while they play. Palettes take the deck colour, live functions their own colour, and the two bottom rows their own hue.

Two ways to press

While held : the effect lasts as long as the press and stops on release. That is the strobe or kill gesture.

On/off latch : one press engages, another releases. Blackout exists in both forms — pick the gesture you want.

Faders : soft takeover

A fader only takes over once it is back on the current value, and re-arms as soon as the value changes elsewhere (mouse, auto-mix).

Brushing a fader therefore never makes a setting jump.

Keyboard

  • S strobe · E electric
  • space flash · B blackout
  • Shift + the same key : latch instead of hold
